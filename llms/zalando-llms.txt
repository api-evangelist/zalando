# Zalando

> Zalando is Europe's leading online platform for fashion and lifestyle. Its zDirect Platform (Merchant/Partner Platform) exposes OAuth2-protected REST APIs for the full article lifecycle: products, attributes, prices, stock, offer availability, orders, sales channels, logistic centers, and Zalando Fulfillment Solutions (ZFS) stock movements and cross-border reporting. APIs follow the Zalando RESTful API Guidelines and use JSON:API and problem+json media types.

## APIs (OpenAPI)
- [Orders API](openapi/zalando-orders-openapi.yml): retrieve merchant orders, items, lines
- [Products API](openapi/zalando-products-openapi.yml): create/update catalog products
- [Product Attributes API](openapi/zalando-product-attributes-openapi.yml): enumerate valid attributes
- [Article Requirements API](openapi/zalando-article-requirements-openapi.yml): UAF requirement outlines
- [Merchant Price Service API](openapi/zalando-prices-openapi.yml): set article prices by country
- [Price Reporting API](openapi/zalando-price-reporting-openapi.yml): price status + history
- [Merchant Stock Service API](openapi/zalando-stocks-openapi.yml): manage article stock
- [Offer Blocking API](openapi/zalando-offer-blocking-openapi.yml): block/unblock article availability
- [Sales Channels API](openapi/zalando-sales-channels-openapi.yml): merchant sales channels
- [Logistic Centers API](openapi/zalando-logistic-centers-openapi.yml): merchant logistic centers
- [ZFS Stock Movements API](openapi/zalando-zfs-stock-movements-openapi.yml): fulfillment stock movements
- [ZFS Cross-Border Movements API](openapi/zalando-zfs-cross-border-movements-openapi.yml): inter-warehouse transfers
